Ordering Process

To place an order, simply browse our collections, select your desired items, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to provide shipping information and payment details. For personal assistance, our luxury concierge team is available at concierge@estherdahl.com.

We process orders quickly to ensure prompt delivery. If you need to modify or cancel your order, please contact us immediately at orders@estherdahl.com with your order number. We'll make every effort to accommodate your request if the order hasn't been shipped.

Yes, our personal shopping service is available for clients seeking expert guidance. Contact our concierge team to schedule a virtual consultation where we'll discuss your style preferences, needs, and recommend pieces from our collection.

Shipping & Delivery

We offer several shipping options to suit your needs:

  • Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days (domestic)
  • Express Shipping: 1-2 business days (domestic)
  • International Shipping: 5-10 business days to most countries
  • White Glove Service: Available for select items with personalized delivery

Yes, we ship to over 60 countries worldwide. International orders may be subject to customs fees, import duties, and taxes which are the responsibility of the customer. We recommend checking with your local customs office for potential fees before placing your order.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your package directly through the carrier's website using the provided tracking number. For additional assistance, our client services team is available to help.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for store credit or exchange. Items must be unused, in original condition, with all tags and packaging intact. Final sale items cannot be returned. For complete details, please visit our Returns Policy page.

To initiate a return or exchange, please contact our client services team at returns@estherdahl.com with your order number and reason for return. We'll provide instructions and a prepaid shipping label for your convenience.

We carefully inspect all items before shipment. If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately at service@estherdahl.com with photos of the issue. We will arrange for expedited replacement at no cost to you.

Product Care

Each Esther Dahl piece comes with specific care instructions based on its materials. Generally:

  • Store in the provided dust bag when not in use
  • Keep away from direct heat and sunlight
  • Clean with a soft, dry cloth
  • Use specialized leather conditioner as needed
For detailed care instructions, please refer to our Care Guide.

Yes, we offer professional cleaning and restoration services for all Esther Dahl handbags. Contact our care specialists at care@estherdahl.com to discuss your needs and receive a personalized service recommendation.

To prevent color transfer from clothing:

  • Apply a leather protector spray (test on small area first)
  • Avoid carrying your bag against dark or new denim
  • Clean the surface regularly with a soft cloth
  • Store with tissue paper to prevent surface contact

Authenticity & Craftsmanship

Every Esther Dahl handbag comes with:

  • A unique authenticity card with serial number
  • Certificate of craftsmanship
  • Signature dust bag and packaging
You can verify your bag's authenticity by registering it on our website or contacting our authenticity team at authenticity@estherdahl.com.

All Esther Dahl handbags are meticulously crafted by our master artisans in Florence, Italy. We use traditional techniques passed down through generations combined with modern precision to create each piece.

We source only the finest materials:

  • Italian full-grain leathers
  • Ethically-sourced exotic skins (from certified suppliers)
  • 24k gold-plated hardware
  • Luxury suede and silk linings
Each material is carefully selected for quality, durability, and beauty.
