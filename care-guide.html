Luxury Care Guide

Preserving the beauty of your Esther Dahl handbag for generations

Esther Dahl Craftsmanship Promise

Each handbag is crafted with the finest materials designed to last. With proper care, your piece will develop a beautiful patina over time.

Leather Care

Our premium leathers require specific care to maintain their luxurious appearance:

Do:

  • Use a soft, dry cloth for regular dusting
  • Apply leather conditioner every 3-6 months
  • Store in the provided dust bag
  • Use a leather protector spray for frequent use

Don't:

  • Expose to direct heat or sunlight for extended periods
  • Use harsh chemicals or alcohol-based cleaners
  • Store in plastic bags (needs to breathe)
  • Over-condition (can cause buildup)

Recommended Products:

Esther Dahl Leather Nourishment Cream, pH-neutral leather cleaner

Hardware Care

Maintain the luster of your metal accents and closures:

1

Regular Polishing

Use a microfiber cloth to gently polish hardware after each use to remove fingerprints and prevent tarnishing.

2

Deep Cleaning

For tarnished hardware, use a jewelry polishing cloth specifically designed for the metal type (gold, silver, etc.).

3

Protection

Apply a thin layer of clear nail polish to costume jewelry elements to prevent oxidation (test on small area first).

Proper Storage

Proper storage is essential for maintaining your bag's shape and condition:

Dust Bag Storage

Always store in the provided breathable dust bag when not in use to prevent dust accumulation.

Shape Preservation

Stuff bags with acid-free tissue paper to maintain structure, especially for soft leathers.

Climate Control

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and extreme temperature fluctuations.

Emergency Care

Quick actions for common accidents:

Water Exposure

1. Blot gently with absorbent cloth

2. Air dry at room temperature

3. Condition once fully dry

Never use direct heat

Ink Stains

1. Blot (don't rub) immediately

2. Use specialized leather cleaner

3. Consult professional for deep stains

Oil Stains

1. Sprinkle cornstarch or talc

2. Leave overnight to absorb

3. Brush away gently

4. Repeat if necessary

Color Transfer

1. Use specialized color transfer remover

2. Test on small area first

3. Gently rub in circular motions
