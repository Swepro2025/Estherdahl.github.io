The Esther Dahl Story

Founded in 2010, Esther Dahl represents the pinnacle of luxury handbag design. Our founder, Esther Dahl, began her journey with a simple vision: to create handbags that combine timeless elegance with modern functionality.

Each Esther Dahl piece is meticulously crafted by our master artisans in Florence, Italy, using only the finest materials sourced from around the world. We believe in sustainable luxury, creating pieces meant to last generations.
