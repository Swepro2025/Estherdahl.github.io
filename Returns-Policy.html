Return Policy

At Esther Dahl, we want you to love your purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, we offer free returns within 14 days of delivery.

1

Initiate Your Return

Contact our client services team at returns@estherdahl.com with your order number and reason for return.

2

Prepare Your Package

Include all original packaging, dust bags, and tags. Items must be unused and in original condition.

3

Ship Your Return

We'll email you a prepaid shipping label. Drop off at any authorized carrier location.

4

Receive Your Refund

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive your return.

Important Notes

Original Tags

All items must have original tags attached for returns or exchanges.

Refund Method
