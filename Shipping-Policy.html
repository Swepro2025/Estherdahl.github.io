Shipping Policy

We ensure your Esther Dahl pieces arrive safely and promptly

Delivery Options

Esther Dahl offers worldwide shipping through our premium delivery partners. All packages are fully insured and require signature upon delivery.

Standard Shipping

  • 3-5 business days (domestic)
  • 5-10 business days (international)
  • $15 flat rate (free on orders over $1,000)
  • Includes full insurance

Express Shipping

  • 1-2 business days (domestic)
  • 3-5 business days (international)
  • $35 flat rate
  • Priority handling and tracking

White Glove Service

  • Available for orders over $5,000
  • Personalized delivery coordination
  • In-person unpacking and inspection
  • Complimentary for limited edition pieces

International Shipping

We ship to over 60 countries worldwide. International deliveries may be subject to customs fees, import duties, and taxes which are the responsibility of the customer.

For international orders, we recommend checking with your local customs office for potential fees before placing your order.

Packaging

Every Esther Dahl piece is carefully packaged in our signature luxury packaging:

  • Hand-wrapped in acid-free tissue paper
  • Placed in a premium gift box with dust bag
  • Outer shipping box with protective cushioning
  • Climate-controlled shipping for delicate materials

Frequently Asked Questions

Address changes must be requested within 12 hours of order placement. Please contact our customer service team immediately at shipping@estherdahl.com with your order number and updated address.

All shipments are fully insured. In the rare event of damage or loss, please contact us within 7 days of delivery (or expected delivery date) at claims@estherdahl.com with photos of any damage and your order number.

Same-day delivery is available in select metropolitan areas for orders placed before 12pm local time. Additional fees apply. Please contact us for availability in your area.
